# PairTrading--Visa-and-Mastercard
Pair trading analysis of Visa and Mastercard using Python, financial modeling, and statistical methods.
# Analysis Report 
## a) Correlation Analysis 
The correlation between Visa (V) and Mastercard (MA) returns was 0.7906, indicating a strong positive relationship.
Because both companies operate in the same payment processing industry, they tend to move together in response to:
Consumer spending patterns
Macroeconomic conditions
Interest rate environments
Competitive pressures
This high correlation makes Visa and Mastercard excellent candidates for a pair-trading strategy, as their price relationship is likely to be stable and mean-reverting over time.
## b) Price Spread Behavior
Key statistics:
- Mean Spread: $171.07
- Standard Deviation: $26.14
- 1σ Band: ± $26.14
- 2σ Band: ± $52.28  
  The spread fluctuates around a stable mean with occasional short-term deviations, consistent with mean-reverting behavior.
## c) Interpretation
- Stability: CV = 15.28%, indicating a stable relationship between the stocks.
- Mean Reversion: The spread stays within ±2σ about 95% of the time, and deviations typically revert quickly.
- Market Behavior: Temporary divergences in 2023–2025 were likely driven by macroeconomic shifts, but the spread consistently converged back to its long-term average.
## e) When the Strategy Works & Key Risks
### Works Best When:
- The industry environment is stable
- Correlation remains high
- Short-term price divergences revert to the mean
### Limitations & Risks:
- Structural breaks may cause the relationship to fail
- Spreads may widen longer than expected
- High volatility can generate false signals
- Transaction costs may reduce profitability
